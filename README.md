# Gorp's World™ Regorped Edition

---

###### What is Gorp's World™ Regorped Edition?
## A Gorp's world like no other. Relive the beloved mod that started it all, rebuilt for Minecraft Java Edition 26.1.

Experience the emotional storytelling and unforgettable characters in Gorp's World, winner of over 200 Game of the Year awards, awarded by me to myself, gorpdev.

In a ravaged civilization (The Overworld), where infected and hardened survivors run rampant, You, a weary protagonist, is hired to smuggle [several hundred? thousand?]-year-old Gorp out of a little hole in the ground. However, what starts as a small job soon transforms into a brutal cross-dimension journey.

Includes the incomplete Gorp's World single-player story and excludes the celebrated prequel chapter, Gorp Overload, which explores the events that changed the lives of Gorp and his best friends Icy Gorp and Spicy Gorp forever (because it doesn't exist.)

## Why you should play Gorp's World: Regorped Edition
### Rebuilt for Minecraft Java Edition 26.1: Tiny Takeover
Completely rebuilt from the ground up using the latest Minecraft Mod Loader technology with advanced visual fidelity, fully integrated Jorp actions and more.

### A faithful remake
Experience the emotional storytelling and unforgettable characters of Gorp and uhh.. in Gorp's World, and explore the events that changed the lives of Gorp and his best friends Icy Gorp and Spicy Gorp forever in the critically acclaimed prequel chapter, Gorp Overload (except you can't because it still doesn't exist.)

### All-new experience
A total overhaul of the original experience, faithfully reproduced but incorporating modernized gameplay mechanics, improved? controls? and expanded options.

Feel immersed in improved environmental storytelling, effects, facial animations (a few pixels dedicated to Gorp's face), and enhanced exploration and combat.
